/about/

About these observations

What you are seeing

Three cameras record east sky, west sky and front-yard views. Timelapses are processed and accelerated; interpolated imagery is not a newly observed frame.

Dates and time

A solar cycle runs from civil dawn to the next civil dawn. Its overnight crosses calendar dates. Hourly stills use local calendar days and show UTC offsets, including repeated hours.

Measurements and interpretation

Station values are measurements with coverage and source times. AI descriptions are labeled interpretations of recorded evidence. Missing values are not zero.

Freshness and privacy

Current camera previews are delayed by at least ten minutes. Raw originals and private notes are not public. Unavailable services and expired imagery remain visibly labeled.

Limits

This archive provides local observations, not forecasts, safety alerts or complete historical climate records.

Failure and lifecycle states

Capturing

Current cycle is still collecting. No completed video is implied.

Queued

Inputs sealed; render has not started.

Processing

Work underway; show actual progress only if measured.

Partial

Available output with explicit coverage and missing intervals.

Unavailable

Dependency or evidence missing. Retry is bounded; no fabricated zero.

Expired

Known historical product no longer retained. Metadata remains.

Embargoed preview

No public image or guessed-URL bypass before the delay.

Stale measurements

Show original source age; refresh cannot make an old value fresh.

Editor expired

Private writes denied. Preserve draft without auto-submit.

Published is shown on the product cards. Analysis pending/degraded/failed is labeled independently of playable media. Placeholder graphics never pretend to be household observations.
