# D16 — Service measurements, product deadlines and pressure policy

Status: measurement definitions and initial operational policies selected; achievable numeric SLOs and resource grants remain unqualified. Requirements R07/R15/R16/R26/R28–R37, §§6.8–6.11, 15.5–15.6 and 18.1–18.2. Consumers WX-18/31/33/35/36/37/38. No production policy, monitoring rule, deletion or workload is enabled by this document.

## What constitutes success

Report process readiness, dependency availability, data freshness and completed product work separately. A ready API cannot make a stalled capture, unreadable object, stale measurement or incomplete recovery checkpoint green. Do not collapse these dimensions into a single uptime percentage. The [acceptance matrix](../implementation/acceptance-matrix.md) remains mandatory; meeting a latency target does not compensate for a privacy, integrity or recovery failure.

Each measurement records product/camera where applicable, immutable work identity, recipe/config generation, scheduled/source instant, durable completion instant, observation time and explicit result/reason. Use UTC for cross-process events and monotonic elapsed time for in-process deadlines; preserve clock uncertainty. Export aggregate low-cardinality metrics; keep capture/job IDs and private topology in protected receipts, not Prometheus labels. Counts and distributions include failures and timeouts, not just successful requests.

The following values are commissioning targets or selected policies, **not measured fleet guarantees**. WX-35 cannot declare them achievable until admitted workload measurements and failure tests support the exact proposed configuration. Keep both all-scheduled outcomes and a separately labeled qualified-input cohort; never remove missing cameras from the denominator to make availability look good.

Latency/availability error-budget percentages and public API/media response SLOs remain material commissioning decisions, not invented numbers.

## Preview retention and source-age policy v1

Use the existing committed sealed-chunk path; do not add mutable-tail reads or a second collector writer for freshness. With five-minute sealing plus a one-minute publisher cycle, healthy source age can approach 16 minutes before scheduling/transport overhead. This is an expected bound under those assumptions, not a measured p99 or a promise of exactly ten-minute-old imagery.

Select a fresh-display target of <=20 minutes source age, stale display above 20 through 30 minutes, and a placeholder above 30 minutes. Missing/unknown timing is unavailable. These are independent of the hard 600-second earliest-publication check, which applies at listing AND asset access for all aliases, thumbnails, HEAD and ranges. Use bounded pointer caches of at most 15 seconds and expose original source/publication timestamps. Old bytes may remain a historical eligible derivative while no longer qualifying as the current preview.

Select a rolling preview derivative horizon of 24 hours from source time, with at most one successfully published generation per camera per minute; do not reissue a generation merely to extend retention. This bounds expected preview volume at 4,320 generations across three cameras, before approved recovery holds and version overhead. Actual byte quota must be derived from measured derivative sizes. Lifecycle expiry withdraws the logical preview from public lookup; physical deletion remains subject to recovery/PITR, references and qualified durability rules. Keep only small committed selected preview inputs while processing; completed/expired inputs release their own pins, never another product's obligations. Outage recovery coalesces directly to the newest eligible frame.

## Resource admission and deterministic pressure

E01/E04 show uneven worker capacity and no Weather grant; E06 supplies three individual JPEG sizes, not p95 cadence sizing; E08 proves generic soft NFS configuration, not Weather mount/durability admission; E10 proves RF1 and mounted Garage metadata, not accepted local-loss behavior. None authorizes workload placement, automatic eviction or deletion. Private resource policy must bind exact namespace/identities, supported submission path, resource requests/limits, admitted concurrency, deadline, quota and owner reservations. Preserve control-plane cordons, camera isolation, resident models and recovery capacity.

Size staging from measured p95 capture payload/cadence, longest segment, measured render backlog/scratch and 30% reserve using §6.8. Capacity is the minimum effective owner grant and underlying available capacity after reserved space, not the PVC label. Track per-camera accounted bytes and oldest required pin so one failing product cannot consume the whole grant. Track bytes and projected time-to-full together; a percentage alone ignores burst rate.

Adopt initial warning/urgent/write-stop watermarks 70/85/90% of the admitted usable staging budget, with resume below 75% only after the failed dependency is healthy and the projected next segment fits the grant. Apply the shared-storage owner's stricter pressure signal as well. Preserve its separately admitted control-metadata reserve; reserve bytes must be selected before collectors run, not guessed from total capacity. At urgent pressure, prioritize already-qualified cleanup and durable compact extraction; suspend optional reanalysis/re-renders and stop admitting new optional work. At stop pressure, stop new JPEG payload writes and report bounded capture loss. Continue independent telemetry where its own admitted spool permits. Never delete unpublished/pinned input to make the graph advance.

A 48-hour oldest unresolved-input escalation is an incident threshold, not TTL deletion authority. Retain exact affected chunk/product identities and the stopped-capture reason. If no safe space can be reclaimed, remain visibly degraded until the owner resolves capacity or an explicit terminal-loss disposition is recorded. A lease timeout, expired preview or missed SLO cannot erase another product's pin. Spool/WAL capacity and protection behavior use their separate owner policies; do not let NFS pressure silently spill dense JPEGs to worker root disks.

Use bounded fair work queues and per-camera accounting. Preview queues coalesce; hourly selection and keogram extraction must not sit behind full flow renders. D10's queue limits are memory design bounds, not admitted CPU concurrency. A motion render may start only if measured remaining-time estimates leave enough deadline budget for the CPU observed fallback plus encode/upload/readback; otherwise choose observed immediately and record why. If even the baseline estimate cannot fit, report the expected breach without silently lowering image geometry, frame count, cadence or privacy requirements. Exact admission concurrency and cutoff need the D10 pilot and remain open.

## Withdrawal and dependency failures

Distinguish natural lifecycle expiry from safety/privacy withdrawal. Logical authorization changes take effect at the origin before conditional responses or ranges; reject stale public revisions and remove current/search projections through the outbox. Never promise recall of bytes already downloaded. Long immutable browser caching conflicts with prompt withdrawal: public media response caching and optional CDN purge bounds must be selected and tested jointly with D14 before production. No fixed immediate global-revocation claim is made here. Private/admin/unripe content is never shared-cache content.

During DB failure, spool within its admitted budget and withhold new publication transactions. During NFS failure, stop unsafe camera persistence and preserve uncertain writer fencing. During object-block failure, report media unavailability even if metadata health is green. During inference/embedding failure, continue measured weather, media and typed browsing. During custody failure, expose increasing independent recovery ages and preserve retention references. Pod/node recovery reclaims work only through its durable generation/fencing protocol. Each failure must have an observed degraded state, bounded queue/pressure response and a tested recovery transition; §15.6's failure matrix is authoritative.
