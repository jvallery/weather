## Why and evidence

A smooth-looking movie can still misrepresent elapsed time, missing captures or lighting. The pipeline must preserve the shared civil schedule, full source field of view, actual capture timing and honest gaps while producing browser-compatible outputs. The observed-exposure reference is mandatory; so is implementation and qualification of a high-interpolation profile. A working baseline alone does not complete WX-14.

E06 verified three individual native JPEGs at 4096 × 1856, not a sustained capture distribution or representative scene suite. D09/E04 establishes device inventory but no Weather CPU/GPU admission. D01/E09 establishes the remaining isolated CI-path gap. All expensive encoding, reconstruction and throughput trials require that supported k3s path and current admission; no local Mac benchmark substitutes for it.

The [official FFmpeg release page](https://www.ffmpeg.org/download.html) and [release index](https://www.ffmpeg.org/releases/) checked on 2026-10-23 still list 9.0.2 (2026-09-18) as the current stable release. Select 9.0.2 as the initial source version. This is not an executable pin: the admitted build must retain verified release signature/checksum, dependency versions, configuration, image digest and capability/probe receipt. Development filter documentation is not proof of a release binary's features. FFmpeg/libx264 distribution obligations remain separate from Weather's MIT license; resolve them in the build provenance review before distribution.

## Deterministic time and exposure recipe v1

Use integer UTC timestamp units and rational arithmetic for interval boundaries, frame allocation, source centers, shutter edges and overlaps. The shared cycle has 7,200 frames at 60/1; round the daytime proportional allocation half-up and assign the exact remainder to overnight. Each segment uses source center a + (k + 1/2)(b-a)/N and output PTS k/60. Never round the source step to whole seconds or derive source position from file ordinal. Every camera uses the same allocation even when its inputs are absent.

Default shutter angle is 180 degrees. Intended exposure is the half-open interval centered on the source center, width source_step × theta/360. Angles 270 and 360 are comparison candidates, not default changes. For an observed capture at t, its support extends toward each neighbor only to the midpoint and at most half the expected capture interval on each side. At a missing neighbor or segment edge the same half-interval cap applies, clipped to the segment. Duplicate/nonmonotonic times and rejected captures have no independent support. This conservative rule leaves holes across missed slots instead of allowing a frame to cover an arbitrarily long outage. It can report reduced exposure coverage under jitter; record that outcome rather than replacing actual timestamps with nominal slots.

Intersect valid support with each shutter. Compute temporal union weight without double counting and divide by the **intended** shutter width. Initial minimum usable exposure coverage is **0.90 inclusive**. Below 0.90, emit the versioned Capture unavailable treatment for that output frame at its original PTS. At or above 0.90, accumulate in linear-light float precision and normalize only by valid support weight; retain actual coverage, including any shortfall. Never label a partially supported shutter as fully observed. Threshold and support rule are commissioning defaults whose changes create a new recipe version and require comparison evidence.

Segment slot coverage remains separate: >=99% normal, 90–99% partial, <90% obvious partial label, and <50% defaults to an unavailable catalog record. Passing slot coverage does not override a failed shutter. A video may therefore contain gap frames even when its slot-level status is normal. No gap compresses time, changes N, or borrows another segment's samples. No synthetic boundary extension is enabled in v1.

## Reconstruction and bounded execution

The observed profile integrates the bounded observed cells above. The motion profile reconstructs at an absolute 4 samples per elapsed second on a grid anchored at segment start; optional 8 samples per second is a separate recipe. At 2 Hz capture those remain 4/8 samples per second, not 8/16. Calculate interpolation alpha from the two actual capture times. Accept a bracket only within one source epoch, inside this segment, with separation <= two expected capture intervals and no timestamp, IR/profile, geometry, PTZ or scene discontinuity. Otherwise use observed support for that region, recording the fallback, or a gap where coverage fails.

For motion integration, use midpoint cells around the fixed reconstruction grid, clipped to the valid bracket and shutter. Each cell is counted once; interpolate before linear-light exposure integration. Retain separate observed-support coverage and reconstructed-support coverage, bracket/source IDs and synthetic fraction. Reconstruction does not increase acquired-slot coverage or turn a synthetic sample into an observation. A renderer must not hide an observed gap merely by reporting dense synthetic samples. The 0.90 usability test uses observed support; qualified reconstruction may improve appearance only within that accepted evidence envelope.

Use the documented CPU minterpolate path as a comparison candidate, not an assumed complete timestamp-aware solution. Its admitted probe must establish that actual irregular timestamps, grid phase, pixel format and bracket invalidation survive the wrapper. If its timing semantics cannot meet this contract, implement an explicitly timestamp-aware CPU flow adapter and qualify that adapter instead. A nominal-fps JPEG sequence, fps duplication, NVENC or hwaccel cuda does not satisfy reconstruction. Record the motion path's intermediate precision; it cannot erase the higher-precision observed reference.

Own output frames in contiguous chunks of at most 60 output frames. Load only their required exposure support plus the bounded in-segment reconstruction/filter context; reuse overlap without changing ownership or phase. Backpressure completed-frame queues to at most two native frames per rendition consumer, with a shared normalized frame stream. These are initial queue bounds, not a memory grant: flow fields, JPEG decoders, float accumulators and encoder surfaces must also fit admitted RSS. At current geometry one float32 RGB frame alone is 87 MiB. Do not materialize a day-long synthetic movie or invoke a process per output frame. Chunk size may decrease under a versioned resource policy without changing output math.

Freeze normalization, masks, noise/exposure settings, reconstruction, integration, scale/deband, overlay and encode configuration in recipe provenance. Default optional denoising, deflicker and stabilization off until measured benefit; preserve true twilight and illumination. Apply the selected privacy mask to every public and model derivative without silently cropping the field of view. The scene-qualified motion default is enabled only after its comparison passes; otherwise publish the honest observed recipe within the deadline. Missing GPU access cannot block that fallback.

## Renditions and keograms

Produce the mandatory native/web/mobile/poster outputs in §7.6 from shared normalized frames. Current expected video sizes are 4096×1856, 1920×870 and 1280×580, square pixels, H.264 yuv420p, no audio. Select actual encoder/profile/level/VBV settings through the admitted encode probe; expected levels are not forced declarations. Both track and movie timescales are 60000, each sample lasts 1000 ticks, start PTS is zero, and terminal duration is N×1000 ticks. A frame-count match alone does not prove mux timing. Preserve all source-time maps and verify full decode, color/geometry, checksums and representative frames for every rendition before publication.

For each camera/segment, v1 keograms use 1,440 equal elapsed-UTC bins and a maximum 512-pixel spatial axis, with no upscaling. For each bin choose the valid observed capture nearest its midpoint, tie-breaking by earlier timestamp then capture ID. Choose only captures inside that bin and segment; a missing bin stays visibly unavailable. Record selected ID/time, bin interval, acquisition uncertainty and slot coverage separately: one selected image is not evidence of continuous bin coverage. Extract the versioned narrow spatial region and average across its width in linear light before spatial downscaling. Never use interpolated or model-generated input. Bin clicks/keyboard selection seek via source time and expose bin precision.

Exact regions/orientation/masks for east, west and front yard remain a required D02 commissioning artifact after private image review. Do not invent a front-yard sky strip or publish the private originals. Retain raw pins until slices, image/thumbnail and axis/gap metadata are durably committed or explicitly accounted for under the outage policy. Keogram failure does not withdraw an otherwise valid video. All six products remain required; composite WX-45 is independent follow-on work.

## Acceptance and remaining qualification

WX-14 must demonstrate half-up allocation, fractional shutter overlap, linear versus gamma averaging, jitter and missed-slot support, coverage exactly at and below 0.90, invalid brackets, no cross-segment context, 1/2 Hz capture with fixed 4/8 Hz reconstruction, and identical frame ownership/time mapping across chunk boundaries. WX-16 must fully decode and validate all rendition timing including the terminal sample and movie timescale. WX-36 must demonstrate all six keograms, midpoint tie-breaking, half-open ownership, missing bins, source-time seeking and cleanup holds. These are test obligations, not executed runtime evidence.

The admitted pilot must include clouds, foliage, stitched seams, precipitation, twilight and night noise; compare observed/4 Hz and optionally 8 Hz at 180/270/360 degrees with a frozen input manifest. Record wall time, peak RSS/CPU/I/O, quality defects, encoder settings and source/build/recipe hashes. Measure both three-output boundary bursts and the six-output cycle with resident workloads and recovery capacity preserved. Derive publication/fallback cutoff from D16's accepted SLO and measured processing estimates, not an invented throughput number. Baseline and high-interpolation quality/performance, exact build/license provenance, calibrated color, privacy/keogram regions, resource grant and deadline remain open. Do not close implementation or planning readiness from this design alone.
